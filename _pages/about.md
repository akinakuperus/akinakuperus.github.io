## Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Mathematical Biology at the University of Victoria, Canada, under the supervision of [Mark Lewis](https://lewisresearchlab.org/) and John Fryxell.

In 2023, I completed a MSc in Discrete Mathematics under the supervision of [Natasha Morisson](https://web.uvic.ca/~nmorrison/) at the University of Victoria. In 2021, I graduated with a BSc in Mathematics from the University of Regina.
